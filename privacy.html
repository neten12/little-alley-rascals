Little Alley Rascals

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Little Alley Rascals is a short-form animated comedy series about Pip, a baby raccoon, and Ink, a black kitten. This policy covers the series' accounts on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube, and the small publishing tool ("Little Alley Rascals Publisher") that the series' creator uses to upload episodes to those accounts.

Who operates this

The series and the publishing tool are operated by a single independent creator in the United States. There is no company, no staff and no third-party service provider behind the tool.

What the publishing tool does

The tool runs on the creator's own computer. Once a day it uploads one episode to the creator's own channels using the official Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube developer interfaces, attaches a caption, marks the video as AI-generated, and posts a first comment.

Only the creator's own accounts are connected. The tool never signs in as, posts for, or reads data from anyone else.

Data the tool handles

  • Access tokens for the creator's own accounts. They are stored only on the creator's computer, in files readable by the creator's Windows user alone, and are used solely to upload episodes and post comments.
  • Basic account details returned by the platforms when a token is issued, such as the account's display name and numeric id, used to confirm the token belongs to the right channel.
  • Upload records: the id and link of each published episode, kept in a local log so the same episode is not posted twice.

The tool does not collect, store or process any information about viewers, followers or commenters. It does not read comments, messages, follower lists or analytics.

Data about viewers

When you watch, like, comment on or share an episode, that activity is handled by the platform you are using under that platform's own privacy policy. The creator sees only what those platforms normally show to any account owner, such as public comments and aggregate view counts.

Sharing and selling

No data handled by the tool is sold, rented or shared with anyone. Tokens and logs never leave the creator's computer except as part of the upload requests sent directly to the platform that issued the token.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until revoked. The creator can revoke them at any time from each platform's security settings, after which the tool can no longer post. Upload logs are kept for as long as the series runs. To request deletion of any data, use the contact below.

Children

The episodes are general-audience entertainment and are not directed at children under 13. The tool does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
