Pip & Ink

Little Alley Rascals

A daily short-form comedy about two tiny alley thieves who are very bad at it.

Pip is a baby raccoon with big plans. Ink is a black kitten who has to live with them. New episode every day on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. Every episode is made with AI and labelled as such.
